**Bellchain** is a visual builder for crypto alerts. Drag building blocks onto a board, wire them into a rule chain, and Bellchain only pings you when everything lines up: price, RSI, EMA, MACD, volume, time windows. Before a chain goes live, the replay shows how often it would have fired over the last 90 days.

### How it works

1. **Drag.** Pick a candle source, indicators and conditions from 25 building blocks, or start with a template.
2. **Connect and test.** Wire them together. The replay runs the chain over 90 days of real Binance prices and lists every alert it would have sent.
3. **Activate.** Get alerts via Telegram, push or email. Each action rings at most every 4 hours unless you change it.

### Built in the open

- **Same rules for every block.** Our own building blocks follow the same node specification that community blocks will use.
- **Double-checked math.** RSI, EMA, MACD and friends are tested against independently calculated reference values.
- **Chains are JSON.** Export a chain, share it, import it.
- **Coming soon:** the node specification, the official building blocks and the templates as MIT-licensed repositories right here, with discussions for block requests and templates.

### Good to know

- Bellchain is **free during the beta**. Every new account gets 14 days of every feature, no credit card, no API keys.
- Prices come from Binance, more exchanges will follow.
- Bellchain sends alerts. It doesn't trade, hold funds or give investment advice.
